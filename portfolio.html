Personal Information

Designer, occasional illustrator, full-time process nerd

I run an independent design practice out of Dhaka, working directly with founders and in-house teams rather than through an agency layer. That keeps feedback loops short and lets every project stay close to the people who'll actually use the work.

My tools of choice are Figma for interface and system work, Adobe Illustrator for identity and print, and a plain notebook for the strategy conversations that happen before either.
