What I'm building toward, and why it matters

Mission

To give every founder and small team access to the kind of considered, research-backed design that's usually reserved for companies ten times their size — without the agency overhead or the six-week onboarding.

Vision

A future where good design isn't a late-stage luxury but the first decision a new product makes — because clarity, from day one, is cheaper than fixing confusion later.
