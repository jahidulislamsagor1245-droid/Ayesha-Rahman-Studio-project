Biography

From architecture lectures to interface reviews

I trained in architecture before I ever opened a design tool — a background that still shapes how I think about hierarchy, structure and the way people move through a space, physical or digital. After three years drafting building plans, I moved into visual identity work for a Dhaka agency, then went independent in 2019.

Since then I've worked with first-time founders sketching a brand on a napkin and with product teams shipping their fourth redesign. What stays constant is the process: understand the problem in the client's own words before I offer a single opinion in mine.

Outside client work, I mentor junior designers through a small Dhaka-based collective, and I still keep a drafting table in the studio — old habits.
